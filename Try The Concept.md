# 🧪 Try The Concept

This isn't the runnable system — it's a way to think through a niche the same way Gold Gap Finder does, so you can feel whether the approach is useful before you pay for either the Quick Scan or the full system.

Take a niche you're curious about and work through these questions honestly, on paper or with any AI assistant:

**Niche:**
**Customer:**
**Geography:**

1. What problem appears repeatedly for this customer — not once, but as an ongoing pattern?
2. What are they doing about it today instead of using a proper solution? (Spreadsheet, WhatsApp, hiring someone, ignoring it?)
3. Who already solves this, even partially — and how well?
4. Where do the existing solutions seem to fall short, specifically?
5. What single piece of evidence, if you found it, would make you walk away from this idea?

If you can answer all five with real specifics, you probably have a candidate worth a proper scan. If you're guessing on more than one or two, that's useful information too — it means the idea isn't ready yet.

## What actually running the system adds

Answering these questions by hand gets you thinking in the right direction. What it doesn't give you is verification — real evidence checking, scoring, and a structured judgment call. That's what the actual system does, at two depths.
