# Community Example — Methodology Only

## Niche

**Small business operations**

## Signal

Operators repeatedly mention manual coordination, spreadsheet workarounds and duplicated data entry.

## Initial hypothesis

There may be a workflow problem worth investigating.

## What we would verify

- Is the problem recurring?
- Who specifically experiences it?
- What do they do instead?
- Is there an economic cost?
- What products already address it?
- Where do those products fail?
- Is anyone already paying to handle the problem?

## Decision principle

Do **not** jump from:

`Complaint → Startup`

The intended progression is:

`Signal → Pattern → Evidence → Competitive context → Testable hypothesis`

This example is deliberately incomplete so the paid research system remains private.
